FAQ

Good questions.

What can I delegate?

Creative, marketing, sales, research, operations, development and many other tasks. The exact scope and timeline depend on the request and required access.

Do I need to build or configure an AI agent?

No. Describe the work you need done. Centra coordinates the appropriate AI employees and tools.

Can I use CentrAlign for recurring work?

Yes. You can delegate a one-time task or arrange daily, weekly, or monthly work.

How do I track delivery?

Use the task dashboard for statuses, milestones, and due dates. You can also message Centra on WhatsApp for updates and coordination.

Is every task completed in two hours?

No. Delivery times depend on complexity. You'll see the applicable due date and progress updates for your task.
